Academic Accommodations

Virginia Tech welcomes students with disabilities into the University's educational programs. The University promotes efforts to provide equal access and a culture of inclusion without altering the essential elements of coursework. If you anticipate or experience academic barriers that may be due to disability, including but not limited to ADHD, chronic or temporary medical conditions, deaf or hard of hearing, learning disability, mental health, or vision impairment, please contact the Services for Students with Disabilities (SSD) office at 540-231-3788, ssd@vt.edu, or visit ssd.vt.edu.

If you have an SSD accommodation letter, please meet with me during office hours as early in the semester as possible to deliver your letter and discuss your accommodation. You must give me reasonable notice to implement your accommodation, which is at least a week before each exam. If there are aspects of this course that prevent you from learning or exclude you, please let me know as soon as possible. Together, we will develop strategies to meet both your needs and the requirements of the course.
