The controls

Set by the Fed. Moves the money market and investment panels — the whole AD curve shifts.
3Shift it — fiscal policy
No money market effect. G and T never touch Md or Ms — panels 1–2 (money market, investment) stay exactly where they were. Only the goods-market line and the AD curve move.
